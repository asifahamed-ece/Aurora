A Letter for Chandni

Dear Chandni,

If you are reading this, you found it. That alone means something to me — that you were curious enough to look.

I made this little thing for you. Not because I expected anything in return, but because you are the kind of person who deserves something made, not bought.

I won't pretend this is just an ordinary gadget. It is a keepsake for you. For the laughs, the small kindnesses, the way you make an ordinary day feel a little less ordinary.

I hope, when you open this on some quiet evening, you feel a little warmer for a moment. That is all I wanted.

— Asif
