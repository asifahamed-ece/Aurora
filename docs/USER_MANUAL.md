# Hey Saira! This is Aurora

A tiny desk buddy who lives on your desk and keeps you company.
She blinks, smiles, and hums a lullaby for you.

---

## What's Inside

- Aurora (the little box with the screen)
- A tiny charging cable
- This card (hi!)

---

## How She Works

**She's always on.** Just glance at her whenever you want.

**Touch her chin** — she lights up and shows love. Do it a bunch and she gets really happy.

**Leave her alone for a while** — she gets a little lonely. Just touch her and she's back to smiling.

**At midnight** — she has a tiny reminder for you. Check your phone's WiFi for a little surprise.

---

## The Little Button

The tiny button on the side cycles through her screens:

1. Her face (the main thing!)
2. Clock & date
3. A sweet quote
4. A WiFi QR code — scan it to connect
5. A heartbeat keepsake from me

---

## Music

Aurora hums a gentle lullaby. She plays it softly so she doesn't bother you.
You can toggle it from the dashboard on your phone.

## The Dashboard

Connect your phone to **Aurora** WiFi (password: `for-chandni`), then open a browser and go to:

**192.168.4.1**

That's your control center. You can change her daily messages, listen to the music box, and see how she's doing.

## QR Code

Aurora's screen shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone's camera to connect to her WiFi.
