How She Works

  • She's always on — just glance at her whenever you want.
  • Touch her — she lights up with love, heart-eyes and floating hearts.
  • Leave her alone for a while and she gets a little lonely. Touch her and she's happy again.
  • At midnight she shows a tiny reminder — a love envelope with a daily message for you.
  • When she's tired she sleeps peacefully, with little "z Z Z" bubbles.

The Little Button

Tap the button on her side to cycle through her screens:

  • Her cute face (the main thing)
  • Clock & date
  • A sweet quote
  • WiFi QR — "Scan to Connect"
  • Dashboard QR — "Scan to Open"
  • A keepsake heartbeat from me

She breathes softly with a glowing light that changes with her mood.
