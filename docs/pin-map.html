⚠ GPIO2 strapping pin: Do not hold the WiFi button down while the C3 is booting or resetting. The internal pull-up keeps it HIGH at boot — your finger would pull it LOW and the chip might not start. Let go of all buttons, wait ~1s after power-on, then press.

Pre-flight checklist (before you flash)

  • OLED VCC/GND/SDA/SCL wired to 3V3/GND/GPIO6/GPIO7
  • LED on GPIO4 via 220Ω resistor, cathode to GND
  • 4 buttons wired: each button has one leg to its GPIO, the other to GND
  • 10kΩ pull-up from GPIO0 to 3V3
  • Battery divider: LiPo+ → 100kΩ → (100kΩ → GND); mid-point → GPIO3
  • 470µF electrolytic across 3V3 and GND, near the C3
  • No button held down when powering on or pressing RESET
  • All grounds are common (3V3 rail, GND rail, LiPo GND, OLED GND)
  • USB-C cable is data-capable (not charge-only) — you need this for serial + flashing
Common failure modes:
  • Holding a button during reset → boot fails. Press RESET with no buttons pressed.
  • Charge-only USB cable → no serial, no flash. Use a data cable.
  • Missing 470µF cap → OLED flickers, ESP32 resets randomly when WiFi fires up later.
  • LiPo connected without divider → dead ADC, possibly dead chip. Always R1+R2 between battery+ and GPIO3.
  • Wrong OLED I²C address (0x3D vs 0x3C) → screen stays blank. If 0x3C fails, try 0x3D in config.h.
